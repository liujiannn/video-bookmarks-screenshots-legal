Quick start

  1. Open the extension's side panel and create a deck.
  2. On a supported video page, press Alt+M (or your configured shortcut) to bookmark the current moment.
  3. The timestamp and a screenshot of the frame are saved automatically. Click any bookmark to jump back to that second.

Frequently asked questions

Which sites are supported?

YouTube, Bilibili, Vimeo, Netflix, iQIYI, Tencent Video, Tudou, Youku, Instagram, X/Twitter, TikTok and Facebook. Other sites may work partially.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync. The extension works entirely offline.

Where is my data stored?

Locally in your browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open the extension's settings page and use Export to save a ZIP archive. The same page can re-import it later.

The shortcut does nothing on a video page

Click the video page first so it has focus, then press the shortcut. If the issue persists, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts and make sure the command is bound.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Clear data. This removes all local bookmarks and decks and cannot be undone.

支持与帮助

快速上手

  1. 打开扩展侧边栏，创建一个卡组。
  2. 在支持的视频页面按 Alt+M（或你设置的快捷键）收藏当前时刻。
  3. 时间戳和画面截图会自动保存；点击任意书签即可跳回对应秒数。

常见问题

需要登录吗？

不需要。没有账号、没有注册、没有云同步，完全离线可用。

数据存在哪里？

只存在你的浏览器本地（IndexedDB / localStorage），不会上传。详见隐私政策。

如何备份或迁移数据？

在扩展设置页使用「导出」保存 ZIP 备份，之后在同一页面重新导入即可。

快捷键在视频页没反应

先点击视频页面使其获得焦点，再按快捷键；若仍无效，打开 chrome://extensions/shortcuts 确认快捷键已绑定。
